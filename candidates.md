# One-Line Laugh — candidates (drafted 2026-11-24, all original)

Judge criteria (most important first): 1) funny + ≤200 chars; 2) real surprise at the end that still makes sense; 3) everyday truth people recognise; 4) economical, every word earns its place; 5) fresh, not heard many times.

## Candidates (score = funny / surprise / truth / economy / fresh, out of 5)

1. "I bought a standing desk so I'd stop sitting all day; now I just stand here, doing nothing, at a higher altitude."
   3 / 4 / 4 / 3 / 4 — "higher altitude" is a decent turn, but the setup runs long.

2. "My smartwatch congratulated me on closing my rings today, which is generous, since all I did was reach for the remote with feeling."
   4 / 4 / 5 / 3 / 4 — strong everyday truth, good turn; slightly wordy.

3. "I told my plants I'd water them on a schedule, and now they hold me to it better than any boss ever has."
   3 / 3 / 4 / 4 / 3 — the boss comparison is a known trope; the turn is mild.

4. "The printer only ever runs out of ink when something is due in ten minutes, which I have decided is a law of physics."
   4 / 3 / 5 / 4 / 2 — universal truth, but "a law of physics" is a clichéd closer.

5. "I downloaded a meditation app to calm down, and now I get anxious about the notifications telling me to relax."
   4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 — tight irony, fresh; the turn is good but not a gut-punch.

6. "My phone's screen time report arrived like a performance review from a manager who watched me do nothing all week."
   5 / 5 / 5 / 4 / 4 — WINNER. Universal truth (everyone dreads the weekly report), the surprise lands on the very last words ("watched me do nothing all week"), economical, and the "performance review" framing is fresh.

7. "I finally organized my cables, and within a week they had tangled themselves into a knot that would impress a sailor."
   3 / 3 / 4 / 4 / 3 — sailor-knot imagery is familiar; the turn is soft.

## Winner
#6. Revision plan (gate requires a 2nd write): v1 uses "arrived like"; v2 tightens the verb to "reads like" — a report reads, it doesn't arrive, and it saves nothing while sounding more natural aloud.
